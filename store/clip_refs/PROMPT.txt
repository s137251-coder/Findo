An eight-second vertical 9:16 video: a girl becomes a cartoon and disappears
into an illustrated crowd. Silent, no text anywhere in the frame.

THE GIRL, FROM THE FIRST REFERENCE IMAGE
Brown hair in two long braids, a bright yellow sleeveless top with a black
V-shaped neckline, a mid-violet knee-length skirt, black shoes. Same person in
every frame.

0.0 to 1.5 SECONDS - THE REAL GIRL
She stands on a plain white background, framed from the knees up, evenly lit.
She looks into the camera, smiles, and gives one small friendly wave. Camera
still.

1.5 to 2.5 SECONDS - SHE BECOMES A DRAWING
She turns into the flat cartoon in the SECOND reference image. The change
sweeps up her body once, feet to head, in under a second. By 2.5 seconds she is
completely and only a flat drawing: one uniform black outline of even weight,
solid flat colour fills, a simple face with small dot eyes. No skin shading, no
strands of hair, no folds in the fabric, no soft edges, no highlights anywhere
on her. If any part of her still looks photographed after 2.5 seconds, the shot
is wrong.

2.5 to 4.0 SECONDS - THE WORLD ARRIVES
The white background is replaced by the illustrated funfair from the THIRD
reference image, in the same flat style: stalls, bunting, a carousel, a big
wheel, seen from a high three-quarter aerial angle about 55 degrees looking
down. The camera rises and pulls back while she walks away from it into the
fair. Cartoon people are already around her and more arrive.

4.0 to 8.0 SECONDS - SHE BECOMES ONE OF THE CROWD
The camera keeps rising and pulling back until the whole fair fills the frame
with hundreds of small people. As it rises, she shrinks with the scene like
everything else. The last second holds completely still.

HOW SHE MUST LOOK IN THAT LAST SHOT - THIS IS THE POINT OF THE WHOLE CLIP
She is one of the crowd and nothing more:

- HER SIZE: exactly the same height as every other person in the frame, about
  one thirtieth of the image height, as in the FOURTH reference image. Not
  taller. Not nearer the camera. If she is bigger than the people beside her,
  the shot is wrong.
- AROUND HER: other people standing close on every side, within one body width,
  the same as anywhere else in the crowd. No clearing, no gap, no empty ground
  around her, and she is not the only person in her part of the fair.
- WHERE SHE IS: not in the middle of the frame. About a third of the way in
  from one edge, among stalls and people.
- HOW SHE IS DRAWN: exactly like everyone else - same outline weight, same flat
  fills, same simple face, same proportions. Her yellow and her violet are
  ordinary colours in this crowd, not brighter than the rest.
- FACING: away from the camera, standing still, doing nothing that draws the
  eye.

A viewer should have to search for several seconds to find her. If she is
obvious in the first second, the clip has failed.

THE OTHER RULE
Nobody else may wear all three of her signatures together. Other people may
have brown braids, or a yellow top, or a violet skirt, but never two on one
person and never all three. Plenty of the crowd wear yellow of other shades and
purple of other shades, so those colours are ordinary here.

Nothing points at her, circles her, lights her or marks her. No other character
looks at the camera.

DO NOT INCLUDE
No text, letters, numbers, signs, logos or watermarks. No arrows, circles,
sparkles or highlights. No camera shake, no speed ramps. No music or speech.
